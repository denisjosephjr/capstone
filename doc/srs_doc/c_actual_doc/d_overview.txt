Product Functions
    Our product with be a website and it starts by to accepting a file
    (.docx, .pdf, or .txt) as an input.

    The inputed file will be matched to files collected in our student submission 
    database. Title page and sources segment gets discarded and the main body of 
    text is tokenized to then compared with the database.

    Upon comparison a similarity percentage is given with sources. Depending on 
    the instructor's percentage threshold, the assignment is accepted or denied.
    
    The student's assignment is then added to our database.
    

Operating Environment
    A stable internet connection is needed as far as a network environment is
    concerned. The website will work best on a browser on a computer. 

    Mobile browser support is not guarenteed because of potential formatting
    issues.


Design and Implementation Contraints
    The AI model that may be used to compare student submission and the database 
    of student submissions are the primary contraints. 

    FERPA laws prevent us from buildling a large database of student assignments
    to train an AI model. The database will be curated by our team and not 
    sourced. 

    Building an AI model that could perform such a task will be difficult in 
    terms of avoiding self plagerism pitfalls and privacy concerns.
    
    The specific model type: 
    Artificial Intelligence -> Machine Learning -> Deep Neural Network -> 
    Transformer Model -> Detector.


Assumptions and Dependencies
    Assuming that an instructor or student will be using our service on a 
    computer with a stable browser and internet connection.

    The assumed file types of the assignments will be .pdf, .docx, and .txt

    As for dependencies, the frontend will use React.

    For AI text detection we will use RoBERTa by OpenAI.
