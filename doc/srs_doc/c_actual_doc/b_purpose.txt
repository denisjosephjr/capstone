=================================================================================================

The main purpose of the Intelligent Assignment Similarity and Derivation Analysis system,
otherwise known as IAS-DAS, is intended to detect similarities in a students programming 
assignment, and any kind of written report that could have been produced from paraphrasing,
reorganizing text/logic structure,and even goes as deep as being able to detect AI assisted 
information. The IAS-DAS will be able to flag these assignments by using multiple different 
techniques that focus on the structural semantics of the submitted work. If the IAS-DAS has 
gone through the steps to verify that the submitted assignment was in fact not done by the 
student that submitted it, it will let the instructor determine how to proceed since academic 
integrity had been broken.
