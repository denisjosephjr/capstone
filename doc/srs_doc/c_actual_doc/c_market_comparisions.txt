As far as plagerism detection tools go in the academic field, Turnitin is 
arguably the titan of the field. Turnitin is partnered with over 20,000 
institutions and has a repo containing over 1.8 billion student submissions. 

Turnitin is an instructor facing tool that is not student friendly. An individual
cannot make an account on their website and use it individually. They rather sell
licenses to institutions. Self plagerism can also be a big problem for previous
submissions.

There are other tools such as Scibbr with an Onyx text matching engine that is 
good for students to check their own work.

iThenticate (owned by Turnitin's parent company, Advance Publications) is good 
academic researchers and journalists.

Copyleaks and GPT Zero are AI based checkers.

Grammarly is easy to use, mainly for grammar mistakes but it has a built in 
plagerism detector.


Overall Turnitin dominates, but can work more to be student friendly and have 
better error handling with self plagerism.
